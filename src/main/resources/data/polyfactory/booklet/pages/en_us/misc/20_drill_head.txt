<yellow>Drill Head</> is an item which can be used to mine blocks. It can't be used on its own and needs to be put into a
<u><blue><pagelink '/rotation/80_mechanical_miner'><item 'polyfactory:miner'></*> or a <u><blue><pagelink './21_portable_drill'><item 'polyfactory:portable_drill'></*>.
<nl2>
Drill Heads act as a replacement for both pickaxe and shovel and exist for (compatible) respective pickaxes made from Copper or better materials.
They also have higher durability (x2.5 as durable for metal tiers, x2.25 as durable for Diamond / Netherite tiers) and mine 50% faster.
<nl>
Drill Head can be enchanted and accepts the same ones as regular Pickaxe.
