<yellow><item 'polyfactory:portable_drill'></> is an item which can be used to mine blocks when combined
with <u><blue><pagelink './drill_head'>a Drill Head</*>, taking its properties.
It can't break blocks at all without a Drill Head or without a <pageref '/fluids/portable_fluid_tank'> filled with Biodiesel.
<nl2>
By right-clicking you can change it's mining mode, with them being:
- Single - Mines a single block.
- Area 2x2 - Mines 2x2x1 area based on where player breaks a block. Requires 3x more fuel than single mode for single use.
- Area 3x3 - Mines 3x3x1 area based on where player breaks a block. Requires 5x more fuel than single mode for a single use.
<nl2>
It also has lower mining range than regular tools such as pickaxe or shovel.
