<yellow><item 'polyfactory:fermenter'></> is a 1x2x1 machine block that allows to ferment certain items into other items and fluids.
<nl2>
This block can process up to 6 items at the same time, while being able to hold 6 stacks out output items and 2 buckets of output fluids.
As such fluids can only be pulled out of it and not put back in.
<nl>
This block is primarily used in creation of <yellow><lang 'fluid_type.polyfactory.ethanol'></*>, <u><blue><polydex 'polyfactory:ethanol' usage>which then can be used for further recipes</*>.
<nl2>
This block doesn't require rotational energy to work, but providing it will make it work faster. It's stress requirement is always equal to 5 units when active.
<nl>
Conveyors can also directly pull from / push to this block, as long they are oriented correctly.
